Tokyo Travel Guide

Tokyo is the capital city of Japan.

Shibuya Crossing is one of Tokyo's most famous landmarks.
It is known for its extremely busy pedestrian crossing and
large shopping and entertainment areas.

Senso-ji is a historic Buddhist temple located in Asakusa.
It is one of Tokyo's most popular cultural attractions.

Tokyo Skytree is a broadcasting and observation tower.
Visitors can see a large portion of Tokyo from its observation decks.

Meiji Shrine is located near Harajuku.
It is surrounded by a large forest and is dedicated to Emperor Meiji
and Empress Shoken.

Tokyo is well connected by trains and subway systems.
The JR Yamanote Line connects several important areas of the city.
